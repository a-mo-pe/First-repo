JOLLOF RICE RECIPE

Jollof Rice is a popular West African one-pot rice dish cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper base

Ingredients

  • Tomato Paste
  • Grounded Pepper MiX(Tomatoes, tomato paste, onions, chili or bell peppers etc.)
  • Seasonings & Stock(Curry powder, dried thyme, bay leaves, ginger, garlic, seasoning cubes, and meat or fish broth)
  • Rice(Parboiled long-grain rice or Golden Sella basmati)

Instructions

  1. Pour your preferred paste tomatoes into hot oil and fry properly
  2. pour your grounded pepper mix and fry for atleast 5min
  3. Add your seasonings and stock boil for some minuetes
  4. Add water and boil for 5min,add your washed rice and stir under low heat
  5. Check if soften(if it's not soften,add water by little till its soften)
  6. Serve with chicken, goat, beef, or fish alongside fried plantains (dodo), moi-moi, or salad.
